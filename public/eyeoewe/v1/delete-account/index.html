Account deletion

Version 1.0 · Last updated 28 August 2026

Delete your account from the app without sending credentials through a web form.

Privacy policy Support

Delete in the app

  1. Open Eye O Ewe and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Account & privacy.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm the destructive action.

The flow requires an online authenticated session. If the account still owns an active group, transfer ownership to another active member or delete that group first.

What the action removes

  • The private profile and Supabase authentication record.
  • Outstanding invitations created by the account.
  • Active memberships for the account.
  • The account’s local profile, session, cached groups, pending changes, and conflicts.

What remains

Shared group history remains mathematically valid. Historical entries and actor references retain the exact display label Deleted user. The group itself remains while it exists; its owner can delete it separately from Group settings.

This page does not collect a deletion request, password, one-time code, invitation code, session token, or exported ledger. If you need help reaching the in-app control, use Support.

For the complete implemented data boundary, read the privacy policy.
